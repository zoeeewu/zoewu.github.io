project_summary = """
This work was part of a broader project investigating the CtBP–TRIM28 complex and its role in autophagy-related disease mechanisms. My contribution focused on metabolic and adipose models. I was particularly interested in a simple question: **when myogenic cells lose their normal muscle fate, can they be pushed toward adipogenesis and eventually contribute to fat infiltration in muscle?**

To explore this, MyoD-deficient C2C12 cells were induced toward a brown adipocyte phenotype and examined using markers such as PPARγ and UCP1. The project also extended this question into an in vivo setting. High-fat-diet mouse models were used to study diabetes-associated metabolic changes, while I performed blood glucose monitoring, assisted with mouse dissection, and prepared paraffin-embedded tissues for histological analysis. Together, these experiments linked changes in cell fate observed in culture with adipose and metabolic phenotypes at the tissue level.
"""
